# Phase II Contributions

For our Phase II portion of our project, I designed the wireframe models and helped develop the ER and DDL models for our database. I used GoodNotes to develop our wireframes and created a home page and multiple pages for each user persona. For the ER and DDL diagrams, I helped brainstorm entities and attributes, and integrated them into our database schema. 

# CS3200 Class Material

In class, we've expanded our knowledge of relational databases and SQL. We've learned how to create ER Diagrams, Relational Models, and DDL schemas to physically implement our conceptual designs. We directly applied these skills to our second phase of our project and used them to design and build a basic database structure for our application.

# Favorite Parts of Belgium

My favorite part of Belgium during our phase II portion of our project was our trip to Ghent. We got to see Saint Bavo's Cathedral and even experienced a motorboat tour of the Lys and Scheldt rivers. The overall trip was amazing and the views were gorgeous. I will definitely be going back to sightsee and enjoy more parts of the city.
