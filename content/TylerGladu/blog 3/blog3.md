# Phase III Contributions

For Phase III of our project, I helped design and implement our UI pages and connnect our ML models to our frontend. In terms of our UI, I created 3 pages for our Student persona and 2 pages for our Budget Manager persona. Students are now able to fill out survery questions and receive a personalized list of universities based on their responses. I've also added a pros and cons tab, as well as a view more button for each university, but have yet to implement them yet. I also helped connect the ML model to the UI through a connecting method in the backend, which allows the user to input information, have the model process it, and produce a specialized ranking. One of our other personas, Budget Managers, are now able to see an overview in their dashboard and view plans created for each individual university. I have yet to implement data and/or machine learning models to this portion of the project but have drafted ideas on how to do so in the coming steps.
